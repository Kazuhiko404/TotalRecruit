TotalRecruit readout

What the player sees when they press Hablar on an NPC the mod can talk to: how worn down they are, the exact chance the engine will roll against, which route answers, and the one thing standing in the way. Open from disk in Chrome; no server, no build. Source of truth for src/readout_ui.cpp; the rules behind each number are in src/readout_model.cpp (README.md in this folder).

Honesty rule. Every number on the card is a number the decision already computed: the chance is the verdict's chancePercent, the price is the verdict's cats, stress is StressFromState. The card never says whether the roll will pass (the odds are the information; the outcome is the click's), and showing it never starts a hold clock, records an interaction or mints an id.

2. Where it sits

By default the card appears only when the player presses Hablar: it is worked out once at that click and then only follows the NPC (anchored above their head, clamped inside the view). It stands beside the offer window the same click opens, never on top of it, and goes away when that window closes, after 8 s when no window opened, or when the NPC dies or leaves the screen. Nothing is shown on hover or selection unless the player turns on the hover opt-in in Mod Hub, which re-evaluates the NPC under the cursor four times a second and costs frame time.

Who is worth talking to is still marked by the vanilla talk marker (hasDialogue_Fast); that answer is asked as a bystander and remembered per NPC for 2 s (src/talk_hint_cache.h), so it costs nothing per frame.

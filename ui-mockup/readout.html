TotalRecruit readout

What the player sees when they hover or select an NPC the mod can talk to: how worn down they are, the exact chance the engine will roll against, which route answers, and the one thing standing in the way. Open from disk in Chrome; no server, no build. Source of truth for src/readout_ui.cpp; the rules behind each number are in src/readout_model.cpp (README.md in this folder).

Honesty rule. Every number on the card is a number the decision already computed: the chance is the verdict's chancePercent, the price is the verdict's cats, stress is StressFromState. The card never says whether the roll will pass (the odds are the information; the outcome is the click's), and showing it never starts a hold clock, records an interaction or mints an id.

2. Where it sits

Anchored above the NPC's head (their world position projected to the screen, clamped inside the view), so it moves with them and never covers the cursor hint or the context menu. In selection only mode the same card follows the selected object; while a right-click menu is open the card stays on that NPC next to the Hablar button.

The speech bubble is the vanilla talk marker (hasDialogue_Fast) the mod already shows; the card never draws on top of it.

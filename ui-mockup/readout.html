TotalRecruit readout

What the player sees when they select (left click) an NPC the mod can talk to, or press Hablar on them: how worn down they are, the exact chance the engine will roll against, which route answers, and the one thing standing in the way. Open from disk in Chrome; no server, no build. Source of truth for src/readout_ui.cpp; the rules behind each number are in src/readout_model.cpp (README.md in this folder).

Honesty rule. Every number on the card is a number the decision already computed: the chance is the verdict's chancePercent, the price is the verdict's cats, stress is StressFromState. The card never says whether the roll will pass (the odds are the information; the outcome is the click's), and showing it never starts a hold clock, records an interaction or mints an id.

1. The states

2. Where it sits

The card follows the selection: left-click an NPC that is not yours and it appears over their head (the point 20 world units over their feet, projected through the camera after taking out the scene's relative origin, clamped inside the view sideways); deselect and it goes, select someone else and it switches. It is worked out when the selection changes and again every 2 s, so price and stress stay current; every frame in between it only follows the NPC. Pressing Hablar keeps it over the head while the offer window is up; only when it would cover the window does it step sideways to the window's nearer side, at the head's height (never up, never to the top of the screen), and it hides when neither side has room. When the offer closes it stays only if that NPC is still selected. It hides while a right-click menu is open (other mods' buttons flank the menu on both sides) and while the NPC is off the screen. Nothing is shown on hover.

Who is worth talking to is still marked by the vanilla talk marker (hasDialogue_Fast); that answer is asked as a bystander and remembered per NPC for 2 s (src/talk_hint_cache.h), so it costs nothing per frame.

5. The recruit popup

Every Hablar outcome opens this one window: a yes with its price, a price the purse cannot meet, a refusal and why, a refusal the player can act on, a plea for mercy, a rescue. Nothing ends in a notification any more. It keeps Kenshi's own Kenshi_WindowCX frame on the Info layer (title bar and X, drawn by the engine) and paints the mockup's flat art over the client area, the way TotalSparring's duel popup does. Source of truth for src/offer_popup.cpp; what goes in each slot is src/offer_view.cpp.

Rules the view applies.
  • A cats price is always shown with the purse it is checked against. Purse below price: the purse turns red, the red line says how much is missing, and ACEPTAR is hidden (only CANCELAR).
  • Any refusal: the reason in red, ACEPTAR hidden. A remedy the player can act on adds its button (DARLE COMIDA / GOLPEARLO).
  • A free join says Gratis in green; Probabilidad appears only when the verdict rolled.
  • The X in the title bar is Cancel. Money only moves on ACEPTAR, and the price is re-checked at that moment (money.short is now only that last-second check).
  • Haggling (haggleEnabled, cats prices only: never rescue, mercy, field yield or surrender). An OFERTA row with a slider from half the ask to the ask (steps of 10, starting at the ask), the live odds under it, and the purse checked against the offer. Below the ask the amber button reads OFRECER; at the ask it is ACEPTAR as before. An offended NPC shows the raised price and "Se ofendio: hoy no regatea" instead of the slider. The section has a fixed height: dragging only changes texts, colours and the button row. States 2-4 are live: drag them.
